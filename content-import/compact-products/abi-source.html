Brief

A tool to increase blood pressure health awareness and promote positive change in young people with prehypertension.

Measurement

Breathing Guide

Different Form

Abi has an accompanying mobile software that can be used by registering or syncing blood pressure data for users who do not have a wristband device.

Abi application will work with wearable devices to help users with recovery record and measurment.

The wearable device will record health data and transmit it to the background. The device will also guide user to control their well-being with breathing practice and proper notice.

The algorithm will recommend more targeted breathing practice and notice next time.

PPG

PhotoPlethysmoGraphy can isomorphically measure changes in skin's absorption of light, so as to infer changes in arterial blood flow velocity and measure data such as blood pressure.

Biofeedback

is a type of mind-body technique you use to control some of your body's functions, such as your heart rate, breathing patterns and muscle responses.

Positive Feedback

Seeing physiological data evolve for the better leads to feedback learning, and data from PPG will be processed towards positive direction.

Guide to control

Vibration therapy, work with abi app, will act as a therapist to provide physical or psychological guidance to help user control their condition.

Active improvement

Just like learning how to ride a bike, people trained in biofeedback must take an active role in developing skills through the help of algorithms.
