What is -Company?

Three-time Tony Award-winning director Marianne Elliott helms this revelatory new production of Stephen Sondheim and George Furth’s groundbreaking musical comedy, at once boldly sophisticated, deeply insightful, and downright hilarious.

The genders of the leading roles have all changed in the 2022 version

What is -The Difference?

The play was written in 1970. People were much more concerned with people who weren’t married. Now a 35-year-old unmarried man wouldn’t be a source of drama or worry for anybody these days. The theme and idea of being a woman and not necessarily wanting to get married and feeling ambivalent about relationships--that’s all very familiar contemporary reality. How it would actually influence the work not just for this production, but sort of retroactively for the piece as a whole.

What the -Work?

Collage ...... Pop-up

The page as a whole is a comparison of the old and new versions of the COMPANY from left to right, the content is a comparison of the musical content, and the form of the representative style of the two periods is also compared. On the left is the collage style representing the retro American style. The raw edge effect has the feeling of being old. Paper notes and tapes are typical things. On the right is the modern style typesetting, with pop up Windows and bubbles emerging to break through the limitations of paper background, in sharp contrast to the right, which also shows the characteristics of this age
