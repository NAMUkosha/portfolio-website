Melodeck

The surfaces and materials of the city are looked at in a systematic way by skateboarders.

The skateboard, like a musical instrument, can be tuned to resonate most directly with the surface substrate it is stepping on.

#MultisensoryDesign

#Soundscape

Activate city surfaces with sound

Soundscape

Inspired by fixed reed instruments, we use the soundproof wheel and electric skateboard to make the skateboard an musical instrument, so as to show the bond with the city surface.

The music is produced using aerodynamic principles related to the tunnels under the skateboard.

The timbre is changed according to the surface, the same as the original skateboard features.

It’s the wind who finally cause vibrations to the reeds, so skateboarders need to utilse the wind while playing the skateboard.

Just like playing a harmonica, skateboarders can use their feet to control the holes, so as to make different sounds.
